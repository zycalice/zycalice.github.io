# Sometimes, You Have to Connect Theory to Reality/Execution by Breaking it Down

Theories are very useful and needed in many things, especially in domains that need technical solutions.
We often want and need guarantees about if things will work consistently.
High level theories could be helpful in making quick decision rules. In some cases though, these "theories"
are not tested, and they remain *hypotheses*.

When you are thinking or modeling a situation where it is connected to potential messy execution,
you **cannot** exclude the execution difficulty or reality from the situation. If you truly care about 
the impact and the consequence, it is rational to break down the high level hypothesis and start adding more and more 
conditions. 

This should almost be the default in social science context, or understanding situations
involving people or complex systems, which is what prompted me to write this thought.

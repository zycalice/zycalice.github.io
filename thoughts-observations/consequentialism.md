# Consequentialism - by Whom and for Whom?

Over the years I have noticed that I have a very strong bias for consequentialism in some conditions (a bit inevitable being partially raised up in a country with a lot of people), 
but a strong deterrence in other conditions. I want to document the conditions and why I separate them.

* For consequentialism: 
  * self/personal growth/regulation
  * (institutions/governance with 1) a narrow and clear goal *and* 2) clear individual consents) 
* Against consequentialism: 
  * institutions/governance with either a broad, unclear goal *or* unclear individual consents

Why this matters? It has to do with who can define what the goal is for consequentialism. 
When you are dealing with personal improvements, you are often the one with accurate access of your goals and preferences.
In institutions, who set the goal? To what? There could be conflicting interests, power dynamics, and imperfect information.
I'd argue a common human goal is hard to define, generally. An exception is a narrow and clear goal, with all individual consents 
on the means to get that goal. I'd argue this could be hard to achieve realistically.

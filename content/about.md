# Background
Hi! I am Yuchen Zhang, a researcher interested in Trustworthy and Safe AI.
I am particularly curious in fundamental understanding of how AI learns 
representations of the world, and see if we can better use these representations
for safer and more controllable AI.
As a result, I am interested in topics such as representation, generalization, 
interpretability, probabilistic logic, probabilistic ml, logic, and NeuroSymbolic AI. 
I care deeply about social good and believe 
that our research in technology and algorithms should benefit 
the public in a safe and fair way.
